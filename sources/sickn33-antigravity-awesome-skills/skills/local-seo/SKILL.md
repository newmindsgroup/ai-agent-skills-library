# Local SEO

Use this skill for location-based search visibility work.

## Focus Areas

- Google Business Profile optimization
- primary and secondary categories
- services, products, photos, and posting strategy
- NAP consistency across directories
- local landing pages and service-area pages
- review acquisition and review response workflows
- local schema opportunities
- internal linking for locations and services
- local intent keyword mapping

## Workflow

1. Identify business type, service area, locations, and main conversion goal.
2. Determine whether the business is:
   - single location
   - multi-location
   - service-area business
3. Audit the essentials:
   - Google Business Profile completeness
   - NAP consistency
   - location page quality
   - review volume and sentiment
   - local schema
   - local backlinks/citations if relevant
4. Map priority local intents:
   - service + city
   - service + near me
   - problem + city
5. Recommend the highest-impact actions first.

## Default Deliverables

- local SEO audit summary
- prioritized action plan
- local page/content recommendations
- GBP optimization checklist
- review and citation recommendations

## Guardrails

- Do not treat thin city-page spam as a good strategy
- Prefer unique, location-relevant content
- Keep recommendations practical for the business model
- Distinguish local SEO from broader technical SEO when advising
