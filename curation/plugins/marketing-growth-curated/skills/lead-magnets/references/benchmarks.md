# Measurement without invented targets

There is no verified universal conversion benchmark in this package. A useful resource and a measurable plan can be designed before a performance target is defensible.

Define the intended audience, distribution channel, cohort and observation period. Distinguish visits, unique visitors, download clicks, confirmed use, optional inquiries and qualified outcomes. Record raw counts and specify each numerator and denominator. Deduplicate where possible and describe remaining limitations. Rates with a zero denominator are undefined.

With no baseline, begin with an observation period and qualitative feedback on usefulness. Leave the numeric conversion target pending evidence. Assumption-labeled scenarios may support planning, but do not call them benchmarks, forecasts or expected results.

An external benchmark requires the original source, date, sample, channel, traffic intent, offer, denominator and a reason it applies. Provider marketing claims and examples are not sufficient by themselves.

A small sample rarely supports a causal claim. Do not claim an A/B-test winner without an appropriate design and sufficient evidence. For an asset-only request, produce the asset and do not expand into analytics setup, publishing or research.
