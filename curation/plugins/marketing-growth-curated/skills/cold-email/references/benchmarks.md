# Measuring cold outreach

This package supplies no verified universal response-rate benchmark. Do not turn an example, provider claim, or remembered statistic into a forecast or target.

For a measurement plan, define the audience/cohort, channel, observation window, delivery status and deduplication rule. Distinguish attempted sends, delivered messages, unique replies, positive replies, and qualified next steps. State the denominator for each rate. A zero denominator is undefined, not zero percent.

A numeric example may illustrate arithmetic only: 8 positive replies from 25 replies is 32 percent of replies. It says nothing about expected campaign performance.

Without a baseline or a verified applicable comparison, propose an initial observation period and leave the performance target pending evidence. If useful, show assumption-labeled scenarios, never a promised outcome. For an external comparison, record its original source, date, sample, cohort, definition and applicability before using it.

Do not perform research, send messages, or configure tracking merely to answer a request for an email draft. Separate the requested deliverable from any proposed measurement work.
