## Rebuild and installation rules

- Read approved-skill-policy.json before installing skills from this repository or the skills library.
- Select enabled standalone entries or the two curated plugin releases. Preserve system skills.
- Do not bulk-copy all skills/ or sources/ into global discovery. Existing general-purpose installers remain archive tools and do not enforce this policy automatically.
- Keep project-specific capabilities in their owning project. Unknown ownership means preserve the source and leave global discovery disabled.
- Resolve ${HOME} paths locally and recheck plugin cache versions. A plugin update may require reapplying its exact-path disable settings.
- Apply through native skills/config/write and verify. Do not copy the entire machine config to another device.
- Keep publication, connector permissions, billing and credentials outside skill installation.
