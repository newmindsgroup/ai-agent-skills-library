Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-15 08:55:22 AST

## Approved purpose

Support daily business development, negotiation, finance, growth, startup development, business design, strategy, branding, identity and brand management, visual and graphic design, web and app design and development, UX, UI, marketing, sales, advertising, writing and social media.

Keep a small supporting layer for research and evidence, accessibility, privacy and security, quality checks, debugging, testing, document production, measurement and reliable automation. Preserve project and brand sources of truth.

## Selection rules

- Retain a skill because it produces a useful distinct outcome, not because its name matches a topic.
- Prefer an existing qualified plugin before creating another overlapping skill.
- Distinguish behavioral tests, source provenance, dependency readiness and real-work results. None substitutes for the others.
- Keep project-specific providers, frameworks and client instructions in their owning project repositories. Do not guess the owning repository or move files across client boundaries.
- Disable unneeded global discovery reversibly before deleting files. Maintain exact paths and rollback receipts.
- A skill with missing tools or resources is not ready. A generic wrapper with no useful method is not a keeper merely because it validates.
- No public publishing, account creation, outreach, purchases or financial transactions follow from this curation policy.

## Vetted addition standard

Before adding a new capability, check the installed inventory and repository first. Document the uncovered task, publisher and license, access and data flows, required tools, example input/output, failure cases, acceptance checks and overlap decision. Do not add trading, medical, unrelated science or entertainment skills to the global business library without a demonstrated task need.
